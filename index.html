The engine · Underwriting is the product

Anyone can build an app. The hard part is saying yes to the right borrower in seconds.

MitraaPay is being built by people who have run live lending books — credit policy, scorecards, collections and regulatory model governance. The platform is the risk engine; the app is just where it surfaces.

01Soft-pull bureau intelligence

CIBIL and CRIF enquiries run as soft pulls, so checking eligibility never costs the applicant a point on their score. Tradelines, DPD history and enquiry velocity feed the policy layer directly.

02Alternative-data scoring

For thin-file and new-to-credit applicants, UPI activity, utility and rent behaviour, employer stability and device signals build a first score — so no bureau file is a starting point, not a rejection.

03Account Aggregator bank analysis

Consent-based statement pull, salary-credit detection, bounce and obligation mapping, and a computed FOIR — all inside the application journey, with no PDF uploads or passwords.

04Real-time business rule engine

Policy rules and an ML scorecard run in the same decision call: eligibility, limit, pricing and tenure returned in seconds, with every rule hit logged for audit.

05Pincode-level risk & collections

Geography is a risk variable in India, and so is recovery capacity. Portfolio performance is modelled down to the pincode and fed back into policy every cycle.

06Model governance by design

Documented model development, validation, drift monitoring and periodic revalidation aligned to RBI's model risk expectations — built in from the first scorecard, not retrofitted at inspection.

Why now · India's credit gap is a design problem

Hundreds of millions of Indians earn reliably and still can't borrow formally.

Not because they can't repay, but because the industry built its products around a payslip and a metro pincode. That is a design choice — and design choices can be rebuilt.
